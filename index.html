About Me

I am an Advocate, Pattimandram Naduvar, Motivational Speaker, Writer, Tamil Literature and Devotional Orator, Founder of Sigaram Foundation. With a strong academic foundation, including a PhD, I have been honored to preside over 1000+ Pattimandram shows across the world. Also met 5 lakh students, professors, and teachers from various colleges across the world and have delivered motivational speeches. My journey has taken me to international Tamil forums, television debates, and educational institutions as a motivational speaker. Passionate about Tamil literature, history, and social causes, I continue to share my insights through speeches, television programs, and community engagements, inspiring and connecting people through the power of words.
